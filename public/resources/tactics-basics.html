Spot tactics faster

Tactics are short, forcing sequences—checks, captures, threats—that win material or deliver mate. If the engine mentions tactics, there was a concrete resource (like a fork or pin) that you missed or failed to defend against. Building pattern recognition is the best cure.

Checklist for every move

  • Before moving, blunder-check: look for loose pieces, undefended back-rank squares, and checks against your king.
  • On every move ask “What are my forcing moves?”—list all checks, then captures, then major threats.
  • When ahead, avoid automatic trades; instead, search for tactical shots that convert immediately.
  • When behind, prioritize counterplay—set up desperado tactics, perpetual check ideas, or stalemate nets.

Practice ideas

  • Drill 10 puzzles a day, spending at least 3 minutes calculating instead of guessing.
  • Theme-train forks, pins, skewers, and discovered attacks separately until they feel automatic.
  • Analyze your losses and recreate the missed tactic on a board, then find how many moves earlier you could have prevented it.
