Keep your king safe

King safety warnings mean your monarch is either stuck in the center, missing pawn cover, or exposed to checks and forks. Fixing safety normally matters more than grabbing a pawn—once you stabilize the king, every attack you launch becomes more dangerous.

Checklist for safety

  • Castle before move 10 unless a concrete tactic wins; moving the king two squares often solves several problems at once.
  • Do not move pawns in front of your king twice without a clear reason—every pawn move creates permanent holes.
  • Keep the queen and at least one minor piece near your king until the opponent's attack is neutralized.
  • Trade attacking pieces (especially queens) if your king is weak and theirs is safe.

Practice ideas

  • Analyze your last five losses and mark the move where you last had the option to castle.
  • Play training games where you deliberately refrain from flank pawn pushes until you castle.
  • Study common mating patterns (Scholar's Mate, Greek Gift) so you can sense danger before it appears.
