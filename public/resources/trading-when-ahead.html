Trade when you're winning

When you are up material, simplifying into an endgame often converts the advantage automatically. Conversely, trading pieces when behind removes your chance to create counterplay. Engine advice about "trading when ahead" typically means you skipped a simplifying exchange or opened complications that let the opponent come back.

Checklist before trading

  • Count material: if you are up a minor piece or more, aim to trade equal material (pieces, not pawns).
  • Trade active attackers from the opponent, especially queens, when your king is slightly loose.
  • Keep pawns on both wings when ahead; trading pawns makes it harder to convert.
  • When behind, refuse trades unless they open lines toward the enemy king or regain material immediately.

Practice ideas

  • Play winning rook endgames against the engine with odds (start a pawn up) and force yourself to trade pieces quickly.
  • Review classic conversion games by Rubinstein—notice how he trades into simple endings.
  • Analyze one of your recent wins and identify the moment you could have simplified earlier.
